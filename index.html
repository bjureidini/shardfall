A wave-survival arena

ShardfallArena

Monsters crawl out of the edge of the arena in waves. Every kill drops amber shards. Spend them at the glowing boxes to roll a random ability. You have three active slots you cast with 1, 2 and 3, and six passive slots that are always on. Rolling an ability you own levels it up, to a maximum of V. You slowly heal on your own, 2 health every 5 seconds. Every fifth wave brings a boss: the Splitting Demon, then the Hollow King, the Iron Colossus and the Dark Lord. From wave 25 they come in pairs, and the Brood Mother joins them. Collect both halves of a fusion recipe and you can merge them into one stronger ability in your loadout.
