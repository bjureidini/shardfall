A wave-survival arena

ShardfallArena

Monsters crawl out of the edge of the arena in waves. Every kill drops amber shards. Spend them at the glowing boxes to roll a random ability. You have three active slots you cast with 1, 2 and 3, and six passive slots that are always on. Rolling an ability you own levels it up, to a maximum of V. You slowly heal on your own, 2 health every 5 seconds. Every fifth wave brings a boss: the Hollow King, then the Brood Mother, the Iron Colossus and the Dark Lord. From wave 25 they come in pairs. Collect both halves of a fusion recipe and you can merge them into one stronger ability in your loadout.

Use Players to set up two-player split screen, pick saves, and choose who has the controller. The Dungeon and the Labyrinth open once you reach wave 10. The Dungeon is a new maze of rooms, traps, treasure and secret vaults every run. The Labyrinth is one huge maze per floor with no doors: find the exit portal past traps, monster nests and boss lairs, and loot boxes of every rarity. Sandbox unlocks every ability at any level you like, with tools to jump waves, summon bosses and turn off damage. Scores aren't saved.
